\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A jointly trained VQC head changes what the CNN learns (4.7 CIFAR-10 probe points by default); across layouts, encodings, gate types and readouts the cost tracks product order, not the presence of entangling gates. Higher-order heads return less gradient per input to the backbone, and a backward-pass fix removing saturation recovers half the top-order cost; neither is shown to cause the loss.
