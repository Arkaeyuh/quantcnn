\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A jointly trained VQC head changes what the CNN learns: the default head costs 4.7 CIFAR-10 probe points. Across layouts, encodings, a CZ ring and multi-qubit readouts the cost
tracks product order, not the presence of entangling gates. For independent factors that keep their product's magnitude, high order empties the middle of the encoding; our high-order bottlenecks saturate early and stay, and releasing them recovers half the top-order cost.
