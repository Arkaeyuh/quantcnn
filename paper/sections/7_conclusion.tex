\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A jointly trained VQC head changes what the CNN learns: the default head costs 4.7 CIFAR-10 probe points. Across layouts, encodings, gate types and readouts the cost
tracks product order, not the presence of entangling gates. Under independence, high-order products that keep their magnitude drive the mean middle-zone mass to zero (not shown to cause the loss); a backward-pass fix removing saturation recovers half the top-order cost.
