\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A jointly trained VQC head changes what the CNN learns: the default head costs 4.7 CIFAR-10 probe points. Across layouts, encodings, a CZ ring and multi-qubit readouts the cost
tracks product order, not the presence of entangling gates. For independent factors that keep their product's magnitude, high order empties the middle of the encoding (not shown to cause the loss), and removing saturation recovers half the top-order cost.
