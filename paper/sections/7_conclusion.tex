\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A quantum classifier head trained jointly with a CNN changes the representation the CNN
learns. With every head tuned on validation data and every selected backbone read by the same linear
probe, the default ring-entangled VQC head lowers CIFAR-10 probe accuracy by 4.7 points, and
the drop tracks the product order of the circuit's CNOT layout, also on new layouts and under a
second encoding, with predictions recorded in advance. Lowering that order, by removing the rings or by
entangling only in pairs that cancel at zero rotations, recovers most of the gap, and
classical heads on the same products follow it. On a pretrained ResNet-18 the default head
costs 6--8 points at every training size when updates are approximately matched. Without CNOTs the head is
a classical sinusoidal model. In practice: tune each head separately,
probe the backbone, and count a circuit's product order before training through it.
